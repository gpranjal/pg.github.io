I build tools that fit my own hands first.

Personal infrastructure for thinking, writing, and shipping. Some of it generalizes. Most of it does not.

Most of what I make starts as a problem I have at 11pm. The tools live on a hardened VPS, on this laptop, and inside Claude Code. They are mine first; the polish comes later, if at all.

By day I work on enterprise infrastructure at American Tower: identity, governance, and integration platforms. By night and early morning I build personal automation, write design systems, and wire small AI agents that do specific things well.

This site is the first real application of a design system I authored to push my work away from generic AI defaults. Cream paper, warm ink, one terracotta accent. Magazine meets developer tool.
